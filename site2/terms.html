Terms of supply

Version 1.0 · 2026 · Applies to all quotes and orders

Plain terms for custom manufactured parts. They exist so both sides know what was agreed. If anything here conflicts with what we agree in writing for your job, the written agreement wins.

01Who you are dealing with

Parts and engineering work are supplied by Vitanova Consulting doo, a company registered in North Macedonia, acting through Mario Blazevski. Contact for all matters below: email.

02Quotations

  • A quote is valid for 14 days from the date it is issued.
  • It is based on the information, files and photographs you supply. If the part, quantity, material or deadline changes, the quote is re-issued.
  • Every quote states one price including shipping. Nothing is added afterwards.
  • Quoting is free and carries no obligation on either side.

03Orders

An order exists when you approve a quote in writing — email or a message is enough. Production does not begin before that approval. You may cancel before production starts at no cost; after that, any work already done is chargeable.

04Prices and payment

  • Prices are in euro unless the quote says otherwise. Any tax that applies is shown separately.
  • Orders up to €150: payable in full before dispatch.
  • Orders above €150: 50% on approval, the balance before dispatch.
  • Payment by bank transfer. Details are on the invoice.

05Lead times

The lead time in your quote is a working estimate based on the queue at the time of quoting. If something will be late, you are told as soon as it is known, with a new date. Lead times are not guaranteed delivery dates unless the quote says so explicitly.

06Tolerances and appearance

  • General dimensions are held to approximately ±0.2 mm. Critical features can be held to approximately ±0.1 mm where this is agreed in the quote and a test fit is made first.
  • Fused-filament parts show layer lines and minor cosmetic variation. This is normal and is not a defect.
  • Exact colour matching is not guaranteed. Filament colour varies between batches.
  • Where a part is cosmetic rather than structural, or the reverse, that is agreed in the quote and inspected accordingly.

07What these parts are, and are not

Parts supplied are for prototyping, tooling, ground equipment and general non-certified use. They are not certified aeronautical parts. They must not be used in flight-critical applications, in life-safety or medical applications, or in any application where failure could cause injury. If you intend such use, do not order.

Fused-filament parts are anisotropic: they are weaker across the layers than along them, and their properties change with temperature, moisture and UV exposure. Where I design the part, these factors are accounted for against the operating case you describe. Where you supply the design, suitability for the application remains yours to judge.

08Designs you supply

By sending a file, drawing or sample part, you confirm you have the right to have it manufactured. Orders that appear to infringe someone else's rights, or that fall under section 07, are declined. Nothing further is charged if an order is declined for these reasons.

09Intellectual property

  • CAD, drawings and design work produced for your job belong to you once the invoice is paid. On request you receive the STEP file.
  • General manufacturing know-how, print profiles and internal methods remain mine.
  • Your files, parts and photographs are not published, shown to others or used as portfolio examples without your written permission.

10Delivery, risk and customs

Parts are packed appropriately for the part and sent by courier with tracking. Risk passes to you on delivery. For deliveries outside North Macedonia, any import duty, tax or customs handling fee is payable by you unless the quote states otherwise.

11Inspection, faults and remakes

  • Please check the parts within 7 days of delivery and report any problem with photographs.
  • Parts outside the agreed tolerances, or damaged in transit, are remade or refunded at my cost.
  • Not covered: modification after delivery, use outside the agreed operating conditions, ordinary wear, or a failure caused by a design you supplied.
  • Where the design was mine and the part fails in the agreed operating case, it is redesigned and remade at my cost.

12Cancellation and returns

Parts are made to your specification. Custom-made goods are excluded from the general right of withdrawal for distance sales, so an order cannot be returned simply because you changed your mind. Your statutory rights in respect of faulty goods are unaffected. If you are a consumer in the EU, the consumer protection law of your country of residence continues to apply.

13Confidentiality

Anything you send about an unreleased product, design or project is treated as confidential and is not discussed with anyone else. If you need a signed NDA before sending files, ask and one will be signed before you send anything.

14Liability

Liability for any order is limited to the value of that order. Indirect losses — lost production, lost profit, delay to your own project — are not covered. Nothing in these terms limits liability for death, personal injury or anything else that cannot be limited under applicable law.

15Governing law

These terms are governed by the law of North Macedonia. Any dispute is dealt with by the courts of North Macedonia, unless mandatory consumer law gives you the right to bring proceedings where you live.
